I looked through the failing test and found the cause. The retry helper reads its deadline once, before the first attempt, so a slow first attempt eats the whole budget. That is why the second attempt starts with almost no time left and fails straight away. I changed the helper to compute the remaining time before every attempt instead. I also added a regression test that makes the first attempt slow on purpose and checks that the second attempt still gets a fair share of the budget. The existing tests all pass with the change. I did not touch the public signature, so nothing that calls the helper needs to change. One thing to watch: two callers pass a deadline of zero to mean no limit, and the new code keeps that behavior.
